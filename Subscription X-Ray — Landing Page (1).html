FAQ

Common questions

Is my statement actually private?

Yes — parsing happens with JavaScript running in your own browser. The file never leaves your device or touches a server.

Which banks does it support?

Any CSV export with a date, description, and amount column — this covers most Indian banks and UPI apps including PhonePe, GPay, SBI, HDFC, and ICICI.

Can it cancel subscriptions for me automatically?

Not yet — that needs a direct integration with each service, which is on the roadmap. Today it gives you the exact link to cancel in one click.
